# ScreenLayer

A simple tool to watch YouTube in a mini-window that always stays on top.

## The Problem
Tired of constantly switching browser tabs just to keep an eye on a video? Or maybe you just want to watch something while working on a project without your browser taking up half the screen.

## The Solution
**ScreenLayer** is a lightweight, always-on-top player that lets you keep your video visible while you focus on other things. It’s clean, simple, and does exactly what it says on the tin.

---

## Key Features
* **Always-on-Top:** The window stays right where you put it, above your other apps.
* **Minimalist:** No unnecessary clutter—just your video.
* **Lightweight:** Won't hog your system resources.

---

## Settings & Controls
The app keeps things simple, but gives you just enough control:
* **Opacity:** Need to see through the player? Adjust the transparency so it doesn't block your work.
* **Auto-Hide:** Set the window to fade out when your mouse isn't hovering over it.
* **Save Session:** It remembers the last video URL and window position, so you pick up right where you left off.

---

## Installation
1. Head over to the **Releases** tab on the right side of this GitHub page.
2. Grab the `ScreenLayerSetup.exe`.
3. Run it, and you're good to go!
